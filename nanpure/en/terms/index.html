2. The App

The App lets you capture a Number Place board from a photo, screenshot, or manual input, solve it with a logic solver, and review the solution step by step. Key features include:

  • Board recognition from the camera or an image file (processed on device)
  • Manual board entry and loading from CSV and similar sources
  • Automatic solving with a logic solver, and step-by-step solution playback (Step Replay)
  • Support for 9x9 boards, large boards, and merged boards (e.g., Samurai)

Solutions and solution steps produced by the App are calculated automatically from the board data you provide, and their accuracy and completeness are not guaranteed (see Section 9).
