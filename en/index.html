Number Place Solver Master

Paper or screen, just read it and solve.

Take a photo of a Number Place puzzle in a newspaper or magazine, or pick a screenshot or saved image of one you found in an app or on the web. Our own AI analysis reads the grid and solves it for you.

As well as the standard 9×9, it handles large grids such as 16×16, 25×25 and 30×30, and combination puzzles of up to 14 overlapping grids.

Main features

  • Reads paper and screens alike

    Photograph puzzles printed in newspapers or magazines, or simply pick a screenshot or saved image of a puzzle from an app or the web.

  • Large and combination grids

    Not just the usual 9×9: large grids such as 16×16, 25×25 and 30×30 are supported, as are combination puzzles of up to 14 overlapping grids.

  • Follow the solution step by step

    See more than the answer: walk through the solution one move at a time and find out how it is reached.
